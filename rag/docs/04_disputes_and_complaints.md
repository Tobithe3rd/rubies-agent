# Disputes and Complaints Procedure

Document ID: RUB-DOC-04 | Version: 1.0 | Effective: 2026-10-01 | Owner: Customer Experience

## 1. When to raise a dispute or complaint

You can raise a dispute or complaint when something looks wrong with your money or your service. Common reasons are a failed transfer, a double debit, a wrong debit, a fee you did not expect, or a loan problem. A dispute is about a specific transaction. A complaint is a broader problem with the service. Rubies treats both seriously. Raise the issue as soon as you notice it, so the details are fresh. You can raise it with a chat agent, and you will be verified before anything is changed.

## 2. How to raise one

To raise a dispute or complaint, Rubies first verifies you. You give the phone number on your account, and Rubies sends a one-time code. The code is a 6-digit number. It is valid for 300 seconds. You have 3 attempts to enter it correctly. After too many wrong attempts, the code is locked and you must request a new one. Once verified, your session lasts 15 minutes. During that time you can raise the dispute or complaint without verifying again. Rubies never asks for your PIN, OTP, CVV or password.

## 3. Disputing a transaction

When you dispute a transaction, Rubies does not log it straight away. Rubies first proposes the dispute and explains what will happen. Nothing changes until you say yes. When you agree, Rubies opens the dispute and returns a ticket number. The ticket number is your proof that the dispute is logged. Keep it safe. Only one open dispute can exist for the same transaction. If you try to dispute the same transaction twice, Rubies tells you it is already open.

## 4. What happens after

After you raise a dispute or complaint, Rubies acknowledges it within 24 hours. The acknowledgement confirms that Rubies has received the issue and gives you the ticket number again. Rubies then looks into the matter and keeps you updated. You do not need to repeat the issue. If Rubies needs more information, a chat agent will ask. The acknowledgement within 24 hours is a Rubies commitment. It is faster than the CBN guide, which expects a tracking number within 3 days.

## 5. Resolution timelines

Rubies aims to resolve issues within clear timelines. A failed transfer, a double debit or a wrongful debit is resolved within 5 working days. A loan dispute or a fee dispute is resolved within 30 days. A general complaint is resolved within 14 days. A failed transfer is also reversed automatically within 24 hours, separate from the dispute. The timelines run from the day Rubies acknowledges the issue. If a timeline passes without resolution, you can escalate, as the next part of this procedure explains.

## 6. Escalation steps

If your issue is not resolved, you can escalate step by step. L1 is the chat agent ticket, which is where every issue starts. L2 is the Complaints Desk. You can reach the Complaints Desk at complaints@rubiesmfb.example or on 0700 000 0001. L3 is the Head of Customer Experience, at customercare.head@rubiesmfb.example. Each level reviews what the previous level did. Keep your ticket number and any messages ready when you escalate.

## 7. Escalating to the CBN Consumer Protection Department

If a timeline passes without resolution, you can escalate to the CBN Consumer Protection Department. Only escalate after the timeline above has passed. Bring evidence of your first complaint, such as the ticket number and the acknowledgement. The CBN address is cpd@cbn.gov.ng. Explain the issue, what Rubies did, and what is still unresolved. Keep copies of everything you send. Escalating to the CBN does not stop Rubies from resolving the issue; you can still accept a solution from Rubies.

## 8. What to include in a complaint

Include clear facts in your complaint. Give your ticket number, if you have one. Give the transaction reference, the amount in naira, and the date. A Rubies reference starts with the letters RUB, then a hyphen, two digits, a hyphen, and six digits. Describe what you expected and what happened. Say what outcome you want, such as a reversal or a refund. Keep your messages short and factual. Do not include your PIN, OTP, CVV or password. Rubies will never ask for them.

## 9. Handing over to a human agent

Some issues are handed over to a human agent. Rubies hands over when you ask for a human, when you are clearly upset, when a tool keeps failing, or when verification fails too many times. The human receives a short summary of the conversation, not the whole chat. The summary is capped at 1,000 characters and states the reason for the handover. This means the human can pick up quickly. You will be told when the handover happens and what to expect next.
