# Cards, USSD and App Guide

Document ID: RUB-DOC-05 | Version: 1.0 | Effective: 2026-10-01 | Owner: Customer Experience

## 1. Rubies debit card

A Rubies debit card is a card linked to your account. You use it at ATMs, at POS terminals and online. A card has a status. Active means the card works and can be used. Blocked means the card is stopped and cannot be used. A card is blocked when you report it lost or stolen. Blocking is permanent until Rubies issues a replacement. You can see your card status in the app or by asking a chat agent. The card number is never shown in full in chat.

## 2. Activating a new card

When you receive a new Rubies debit card, it must be activated. You activate it in the app or with a chat agent. Rubies verifies you with your phone number and a one-time code before the card is activated. You then set your card PIN. Choose a PIN you can remember but that others cannot guess. Never write your PIN where someone can see it. Once activated, the card can be used at ATMs, POS terminals and online. Activation does not change your account balance.

## 3. Lost, stolen or missing card

If your card is lost, stolen or missing, tell Rubies at once. Rubies verifies you with your phone number and a one-time code. Rubies then identifies the card by its last four digits. Rubies proposes the block and you must reply yes before it happens. The block takes effect immediately after you confirm. Next, request a replacement card. The replacement fee is ₦1,500 and the new card is ready in 5 working days. Report any transaction you do not recognise using the Disputes and Complaints Procedure.

## 4. What a blocked card can and cannot do

A blocked card cannot be used. It cannot be used at ATMs, at POS terminals or online. A blocked card cannot be unblocked; instead, Rubies issues a replacement card. Your account is not blocked. Money can still reach your account through transfers. You can still check your balance and use USSD and the app. The blocked card stays on your record as blocked. If you later find the card, tell a chat agent; it stays blocked for your safety.

## 5. Keeping your PIN safe

Your card PIN and your one-time code are private. Never share your PIN, CVV, OTP or password with anyone. Rubies will never ask for your PIN, OTP, CVV or password by chat, phone, SMS or email. If someone asks, it is a scam. Do not enter your PIN on any page opened from a message. A chat agent may ask you to enter your PIN only in the app's secure screen. Keep your phone locked. If you think someone knows your PIN, change it in the app.

## 6. USSD banking

Rubies USSD banking works on any phone. Dial *000# to start. USSD can show your balance, send a mini statement, and start a transfer. USSD uses the same verification as the app: your phone number and a one-time code. USSD uses the same fees as the app. A balance enquiry costs ₦0 and a mini statement costs ₦0. A transfer fee depends on the amount, as listed in the Fee Schedule. If a session times out, dial again and verify afresh.

## 7. The Rubies mobile app

The Rubies mobile app lets you manage your money. You log in with your phone number and a one-time code. If you forget your password, use the app's reset option; Rubies verifies you with a one-time code before any reset. In the app you can find your balance, your mini statement and your certified statement. A certified statement costs ₦1,000. The app never asks for your PIN by message. Keep the app updated for the latest safety fixes.

## 8. Card and channel problems

Sometimes a card or a channel does not work. If your card is declined, check that it is active and that your balance is enough. If there is no network, try again later. If USSD does not respond, dial again. If the problem continues, contact the Complaints Desk at complaints@rubiesmfb.example or on 0700 000 0001. Tell them what you tried and what happened. For a wrong or double debit, use the Disputes and Complaints Procedure.
