# Fraud and Security

Document ID: RUB-DOC-07 | Version: 1.0 | Effective: 2026-10-01 | Owner: Customer Experience

## 1. What Rubies will never ask you for

Rubies will never ask you for any of these: full PIN, PIN, OTP, card CVV, full card number, password, BVN or NIN. This applies to chat, phone calls, SMS, email and social media. If anyone asks you for any of these, it is not Rubies. Do not reply and do not send anything. Report it to a chat agent straight away. Knowing this list is your best protection, so keep it in mind whenever someone contacts you and claims to be from Rubies.

## 2. Common scams

Fraudsters use the same tricks again and again. One trick is a fake alert or credit message saying you received money that you did not receive. Another is a fake "Rubies staff" call asking you to confirm your account. A SIM swap takes over your phone line so codes go to the fraudster. Phishing links look like real pages and ask you to log in. A fake loan fee asks you to pay before a loan is released. A fake reversal offer says a failed transfer will be reversed if you share details. Rubies never asks for money or secrets to release a loan or a reversal.

## 3. Protecting your one-time code and PIN

Your one-time code and your PIN are private. Never share your one-time code with anyone. Never share your PIN with anyone. Rubies sends a one-time code only to the phone number on your account. Enter it only in the app or on USSD. Do not read it out to a caller and do not enter it on a page opened from a message. If someone says they need your code to help you, that is a scam. Change your PIN in the app if you think someone has seen it.

## 4. How to tell a real Rubies message from a fake one

A real Rubies message comes only through official channels. These are the Rubies app, the USSD code *000#, and the Complaints Desk at complaints@rubiesmfb.example or on 0700 000 0001. A real message never asks for your PIN, one-time code, CVV, full card number, password, BVN or NIN. A real message never asks you to pay a fee to release money. If a message uses another channel or points to another link, treat it as fake and do not act on it.

## 5. If you shared your details by mistake

Act quickly if you shared details by mistake. First, block your card at once; blocking takes effect immediately after you confirm. Second, change your password in the app. Third, contact Rubies at the Complaints Desk email or phone and explain what happened. A chat agent will review recent activity with you. Report any transaction you do not recognise using the Disputes and Complaints Procedure. The sooner you act, the easier it is to limit the damage.

## 6. Reporting fraud

Report fraud as soon as you notice it. Rubies acknowledges a fraud report within 24 hours. A wrongful debit is resolved within 5 working days. A failed transfer is reversed automatically within 24 hours, separately from the report. Give the transaction reference, the amount in naira and the date. The full steps are in the Disputes and Complaints Procedure. Keep your ticket number once the report is logged, and keep any messages or links you received as evidence.

## 7. How the chat assistant verifies you

The chat assistant verifies you before it shares account details. You give the phone number on your account, and Rubies sends a one-time code. The code is a 6-digit number. It is valid for 300 seconds. You have 3 attempts to enter it correctly. Once verified, your session lasts 15 minutes. The assistant never asks for your PIN. The assistant will not discuss another person's account, and it shows data only for the account it has just verified.
