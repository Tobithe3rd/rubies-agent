# General FAQ

Document ID: RUB-DOC-08 | Version: 1.0 | Effective: 2026-10-01 | Owner: Customer Experience

## 1. About Rubies

Rubies Microfinance Bank serves customers with everyday banking. It offers savings and current accounts, transfers, debit cards, loans and a chat assistant. The chat assistant answers questions and can start actions such as a dispute or a card block. It also hands over to a human agent when that is needed. This General FAQ gives quick answers to common questions. The topic documents in this guide explain each area in full, and they are the better place to read for detail.

## 2. Accounts and tiers

**Q: What account types does Rubies offer?** A: Savings and current.
**Q: What tier do I start on?** A: Tier 1. Higher tiers allow larger limits.
**Q: What does Tier 2 need?** A: Both a BVN and a NIN.
**Q: What does Tier 3 add?** A: Proof of address and a source of funds.
**Q: What is the Tier 3 maximum balance?** A: No limit.

## 3. Fees

**Q: What is the fee to send money to another bank?** A: It depends on the amount. Up to ₦5,000 costs ₦10. From ₦5,001 to ₦50,000 costs ₦25. Above ₦50,000 costs ₦53.75 including VAT.
**Q: What is the fee to send to another Rubies account?** A: ₦0.
**Q: What does an SMS alert cost?** A: ₦4 per SMS.
**Q: What does card replacement cost?** A: ₦1,500.
**Q: What does a balance enquiry cost?** A: ₦0.

## 4. Transfers

**Q: How long does a failed transfer take to reverse?** A: Within 24 hours, automatically.
**Q: What does pending mean?** A: The money has left your account but the other bank has not confirmed it yet.
**Q: How long does a double debit take to resolve?** A: Within 5 working days.
**Q: How do I check a transfer status?** A: Ask a chat agent, or dial *000# and follow the menu.

## 5. Cards

**Q: What does a blocked card mean?** A: The card is stopped and cannot be used.
**Q: What should I do if my card is lost?** A: Tell Rubies at once. Rubies proposes a block and you reply yes before it happens.
**Q: When does a card block take effect?** A: Immediately after you confirm.
**Q: What does a replacement card cost and when is it ready?** A: ₦1,500, and it is ready in 5 working days.

## 6. Loans

**Q: What loan products does Rubies offer?** A: The Quick Salary Loan and the Small Business Loan.
**Q: What tenors are available?** A: From 3 to 12 months.
**Q: What upfront fees apply?** A: A management fee of 1% and an insurance fee of 0.5%.
**Q: What are the fees on a ₦200,000 loan?** A: ₦2,000 management and ₦1,000 insurance.
**Q: How long does a loan dispute take?** A: It is resolved within 30 days.

## 7. Disputes and complaints

**Q: How do I raise a dispute?** A: Rubies proposes it and you must say yes before it is logged.
**Q: How long until you acknowledge a complaint?** A: Within 24 hours.
**Q: How long does a general complaint take?** A: It is resolved within 14 days.
**Q: How do I reach the Complaints Desk?** A: At complaints@rubiesmfb.example or on 0700 000 0001.
**Q: How do I escalate?** A: Start at L1, then the Complaints Desk, then the Head of Customer Experience, and the CBN if the timeline passes.

## 8. Talking to a human agent

**Q: When does the assistant hand over to a human?** A: When you ask for a human, when you are upset, when a tool keeps failing, or when verification fails too many times.
**Q: Do I have to repeat everything?** A: No. The human receives a short summary of the chat.
**Q: How long is that summary?** A: It is capped at 1,000 characters.
**Q: Where do I start if I need help?** A: Ask the chat assistant, or email the Complaints Desk.

## 9. Security

**Q: Will Rubies ever ask for my PIN?** A: Never. Rubies will never ask for your PIN, OTP, CVV or password.
**Q: How do I spot a fake message?** A: A real message never asks for secrets and never asks you to pay to release money.
**Q: What should I do if I shared details by mistake?** A: Block your card at once, change your password, and contact Rubies.
