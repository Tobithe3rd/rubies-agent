# Account Types and KYC Tiers

Document ID: RUB-DOC-02 | Version: 1.0 | Effective: 2026-10-01 | Owner: Customer Experience

## 1. Account types

Rubies offers two account types: savings and current. A savings account is for everyday saving and for receiving money such as a salary or a transfer. A current account is for customers who send and receive money more often, for example a small business. Both account types hold naira, receive transfers, and use a debit card. Both can be opened in the app after verification. The account type does not decide your KYC tier; the tier depends on the identity documents you provide. Fees for both account types are listed in the Fee Schedule.

## 2. KYC tiers at a glance

Rubies uses three KYC tiers: Tier 1, Tier 2 and Tier 3. A higher tier allows larger single transactions, larger daily totals, and a larger maximum balance. Tier 1 is the starting tier. Tier 2 needs both a BVN and a NIN. Tier 3 needs both, plus proof of address and a source of funds. As your tier rises, your limits rise. Upgrading is free. This section gives the overall picture; the next three sections explain each tier in full.

## 3. Tier 1

Tier 1 is the entry tier at Rubies. To open a Tier 1 account, you provide a BVN or a NIN. Tier 1 allows a single transaction of up to ₦50,000. The daily limit is also ₦50,000, which means the total of your transactions in one day cannot pass ₦50,000. The maximum balance on a Tier 1 account is ₦300,000. If you need to send or hold more, you can upgrade to Tier 2. Tier 1 suits small everyday transactions and receiving small amounts.

## 4. Tier 2

Tier 2 gives you more room. To move to Tier 2, you provide both a BVN and a NIN. Tier 2 allows a single transaction of up to ₦200,000. The daily limit is also ₦200,000. The maximum balance on a Tier 2 account is ₦500,000. Tier 2 is useful if you receive a salary or run a small business. If your balance reaches the cap, further credits are blocked until you upgrade. The steps to upgrade are explained later in this document.

## 5. Tier 3

Tier 3 is the highest tier at Rubies. To reach Tier 3, you provide a BVN and a NIN, plus proof of address and a source of funds. Tier 3 allows a single transaction of up to ₦5,000,000. The daily limit is also ₦5,000,000. The maximum balance on a Tier 3 account is no limit, so you can hold as much as you need. Tier 3 suits customers with larger, regular money movements. Many customers stay on a lower tier until their needs grow.

## 6. BVN and NIN requirements

Rubies asks for identity documents to keep accounts safe and to meet Nigerian rules. A BVN is your Bank Verification Number. A NIN is your National Identification Number. For Tier 1, one of the two is enough. For Tier 2 and Tier 3, both are required. Since December 2023, Tier 2 and Tier 3 accounts need both a BVN and a NIN. This change applies to new upgrades. If you are unsure which documents you have, a chat agent can tell you.

## 7. Upgrading to a higher tier

Upgrading your tier is free. You can start the upgrade in the app or with a chat agent. Rubies will ask for the documents the next tier needs. For Tier 2, that means both a BVN and a NIN. For Tier 3, that means both, plus proof of address and source of funds. Once your documents are checked, your new limits apply. Your balance stays the same during the upgrade. You can move from Tier 1 to Tier 2, or from Tier 2 to Tier 3.

## 8. What happens when a limit is reached

Limits protect you and meet regulatory rules. If a transfer is above your single transaction limit, Rubies declines it. If the transfer would push your daily total above your daily limit, Rubies also declines it. If a credit would push your balance above your maximum balance, Rubies blocks that credit. In all three cases, the fix is the same: upgrade to a higher tier. A chat agent will explain the limit that was reached and what the next tier allows.

## 9. Documents for opening an account

To open a Rubies account, you need a phone number and an identity document. The phone number is used for verification. For Tier 1, you provide a BVN or a NIN. For Tier 2, you provide both a BVN and a NIN. For Tier 3, you also provide proof of address and a source of funds. Proof of address is a recent document showing where you live. Source of funds explains where your money comes from. Keep your documents current.
