# Fee Schedule

Document ID: RUB-DOC-01 | Version: 1.0 | Effective: 2026-10-01 | Owner: Customer Experience

## 1. About this schedule

This Fee Schedule lists the fees Rubies Microfinance Bank charges for everyday services. It covers transfers, accounts, cards, alerts, statements and loans. Every fee is shown in naira before you confirm an action. Rubies does not add hidden charges. Where a fee is zero, the service is free. Fees may change; the current version is always the one shown in the app or given by a chat agent. This schedule explains what each fee is for. It does not explain how to dispute a charge; the Disputes and Complaints Procedure explains that.

## 2. Transfer fees

Rubies transfer fees apply to transfers sent to another bank over the NIP network. A transfer of up to ₦5,000 costs ₦10. A transfer from ₦5,001 to ₦50,000 costs ₦25. A transfer above ₦50,000 costs ₦50 plus ₦3.75 VAT, total ₦53.75. VAT is charged at 7.5% of the fee. A transfer to another Rubies account costs ₦0. The fee is calculated on the amount you send, not on your balance. Rubies shows the fee on screen before you confirm, so you always know the total that will leave your account.

## 3. Account fees

Rubies keeps everyday account fees low. A savings account maintenance fee is ₦0, so you are not charged a monthly fee to keep a savings account open. A balance enquiry costs ₦0, whether you check in the app, by USSD, or with a chat agent. A mini statement costs ₦0. These zero fees mean that checking your own money is always free. Other account services, such as a certified statement, have their own fees, which are listed under SMS alerts and statements in this Fee Schedule.

## 4. Card fees

Rubies debit card fees cover issuing and replacing your card. Issuing a new debit card costs ₦1,000. Replacing a lost or damaged debit card costs ₦1,500. Blocking a card costs ₦0. When you report a lost card, blocking takes effect immediately after you confirm the action with the chat agent. You will be told clearly that the card is blocked. A replacement card is sent after the replacement fee is paid. Card fees are charged once, at the time of the request.

## 5. SMS alerts and statements

Rubies charges for SMS alerts and some statements. Each SMS alert costs ₦4. You can receive an alert for every debit and credit on your account. A certified statement costs ₦1,000. A mini statement costs ₦0 and shows your most recent transactions. A balance enquiry costs ₦0. If you do not want SMS alerts, you can turn them off so the ₦4 charge does not apply. Certified statements are usually requested for official purposes such as visa applications or loan applications.

## 6. Loan fees

Rubies loan fees are charged upfront. A management fee of 1% of the loan amount is charged before disbursement. An insurance fee of 0.5% of the loan amount is also charged upfront. Rubies offers two products: the Quick Salary Loan and the Small Business Loan. Loan tenors run from 3 to 12 months. For a ₦200,000 loan, the management fee is ₦2,000 and the insurance fee is ₦1,000, a total upfront cost of ₦3,000. Whether you qualify for a loan depends on eligibility checks, which are explained when you apply.

## 7. Services with no fee

Several Rubies services carry no fee. Sending money to another Rubies account costs ₦0. Savings account maintenance costs ₦0. A mini statement costs ₦0. A balance enquiry costs ₦0. Blocking a card costs ₦0. These services are free because Rubies wants customers to move and check their own money without extra cost. When a service is free, no charge is added later. If a fee ever applies to a service you are using, the chat agent will tell you the amount before you confirm.

## 8. Disputing a fee

If you think a fee is wrong, you can dispute it. Rubies will first propose the dispute and explain what will happen. Nothing is logged until you say yes. Once you agree, Rubies opens a ticket and gives you a ticket number. You then receive an acknowledgement within 24 hours. The full steps, the resolution timelines, and how to escalate are in the Disputes and Complaints Procedure. Keep your transaction reference and the date of the charge ready when you raise the dispute.
