# Loan Products and Requirements

Document ID: RUB-DOC-06 | Version: 1.0 | Effective: 2026-10-01 | Owner: Customer Experience

## 1. Loan products

Rubies offers two loan products: the Quick Salary Loan and the Small Business Loan. The Quick Salary Loan is for customers with regular salary inflow. The Small Business Loan is for customers who run a small business. Both are naira loans, repaid over a fixed tenor. Both use the same eligibility check and the same fees. You apply in the app or with a chat agent. A loan is only created after the eligibility check and after you accept the offer. This document explains the products, fees and requirements.

## 2. Amounts and tenors

Rubies loan tenors run from 3 to 12 months. You choose a tenor inside that range. The loan amount is not fixed; it depends on the eligibility check. The check looks at your account age, your average monthly inflow and any loan you already have. The largest amount Rubies will consider in an eligibility check is ₦500,000. If you ask for more than that, the check returns not eligible for the requested amount. The exact amount offered is shown only after the check.

## 3. Loan fees

Rubies charges two fees on a loan. The management fee is 1% of the loan amount. The insurance fee is 0.5% of the loan amount. Both fees are charged upfront, before disbursement. For a ₦200,000 loan, the management fee is ₦2,000 and the insurance fee is ₦1,000, a total upfront cost of ₦3,000. Apart from these two fees, Rubies does not add hidden charges. The interest rate is not stated in this document; it is shown on your offer before you accept it.

## 4. Eligibility requirements

The eligibility check uses set requirements. Your account must be at least 30 days old. Your average monthly inflow must be at least ₦100,000. You must not already have an active loan. Your KYC tier must be Tier 1, Tier 2 or Tier 3; all three tiers are allowed. The requested amount must not exceed ₦500,000. The requested amount must not exceed your average monthly inflow multiplied by 3. You must meet every requirement to get an eligible result.

## 5. How the eligibility check works

The eligibility check is rule-based. It reads your account age, your average monthly inflow, any existing loan, your KYC tier and the amount you asked for. It then returns one of three results: eligible, not eligible, or needs review. Each result comes with a plain reason. The check is not a loan offer and it is not an approval. It only shows whether you would pass the rules today. A real offer can still be declined after the check.

## 6. Documents and information needed

To apply for a Rubies loan, you need your phone number for verification. Depending on your KYC tier, you may need a BVN and a NIN; the Account Types and KYC Tiers document explains the documents for each tier. You need an open account with regular inflow, because the check reads your account history. No separate bank statement is needed for the check. A chat agent may ask what the loan is intended for. Keep your KYC documents current.

## 7. Applying for NGN 200,000

If you ask for ₦200,000, the check would require the following. Your account must be at least 30 days old. Your average monthly inflow must be at least ₦100,000. You must not already have an active loan. Your KYC tier must be Tier 1, Tier 2 or Tier 3. The amount must not exceed ₦500,000, which ₦200,000 does not. The amount must not exceed your average monthly inflow multiplied by 3, so your monthly inflow must be at least one third of ₦200,000. Passing the check is not a promise of approval.

## 8. Repayment and loan status

If you have an active loan, you can see its status with a chat agent. Rubies shows the outstanding balance in naira and the next repayment date and amount. You repay the loan over the tenor you chose. Repaying on time keeps your account in good standing. If you think you will miss a repayment, contact a chat agent early. You can check your loan status at any time. The status shows whether the loan is active, completed or defaulted.

## 9. Loan disputes and charges

If you disagree with a loan charge, you can dispute it. Rubies first proposes the dispute and you must say yes before it is logged. A loan dispute or a fee dispute is resolved within 30 days. A failed transfer or a wrongful debit follows a shorter timeline and is resolved within 5 working days. The full steps and escalation levels are in the Disputes and Complaints Procedure. Keep your ticket number once the dispute is logged.
